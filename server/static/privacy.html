Privacy policy

Effective 9 October 2026

gepard.fit turns Garmin training data into readiness, plans and coaching. This policy covers the website and the iOS and Android apps. For questions or privacy requests, email support@gepard.fit.

Data we process

  • Account details: email address, password hash, role and account settings.
  • Fitness and health data you import from Garmin, including activities, heart rate, HRV, sleep, stress, readiness, body metrics and training load.
  • Plans, races, workouts, coach links, notes, feedback and AI-coach conversations.
  • Technical data needed for security and operation, such as IP address, device/browser information, sign-in attempts and sync timestamps.

Garmin connection

Your Garmin email, password and MFA code are used only to complete the sign-in you request. They are not stored. The resulting Garmin access token is encrypted at rest and can be revoked from Data sources. The direct connection is unofficial and may stop working if Garmin changes its service. You can instead import Garmin’s official export.

How data is used and shared

We use the data to provide sync, charts, readiness, plans, workout delivery, account security and support. When AI coaching is enabled, compact training summaries and your prompt are sent to the configured AI provider (OpenAI or Anthropic); raw Garmin exports are not sent. Account email may be handled by Resend. Hosting providers process encrypted-in-transit traffic and stored data on our behalf. Garmin receives requests needed to sync your account and workouts. We do not sell personal data or use health data for advertising.

Storage, security and retention

Traffic uses HTTPS. Passwords are scrypt-hashed, sessions are signed, and Garmin tokens are encrypted. The mobile app keeps its session in the iOS Keychain or Android Keystore-backed encrypted storage. Data remains while your account exists and may remain briefly in rotating backups. Deleting your account in Settings removes the live account, imported history, plans, conversations and connections; backups expire according to the backup schedule.

Your choices and rights

You can disconnect Garmin, rotate sync credentials, sign out other devices, edit account details and delete the account inside the product. Depending on where you live, you may also request access, correction, export, restriction or deletion by contacting us. You may withdraw notification permission in your device settings.

Health notice and children

Readiness and coaching are informational and are not medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. The service is not directed to children under 16.

Changes

Material changes will be reflected here with a new effective date. Continued use after an update means the new policy applies.
