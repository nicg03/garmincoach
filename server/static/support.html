Support

For help with the website or mobile app, email support@gepard.fit. Include the device, operating-system version and the page where the problem occurred. Never send your Garmin password, MFA code or sync token.

Garmin will not connect

Try reconnecting from Settings → Data sources. Garmin may temporarily block sign-ins from the server. Your official Garmin export ZIP remains the reliable fallback and can be imported from the same screen.

Data looks out of date

Open Data sources and check the last sync and any error shown there. A new direct sync can take a minute; older history is filled progressively.

AI coaching is unavailable

The coach depends on the service provider and a per-account daily limit. Your Garmin data, charts and plans remain available when AI coaching is off.

Notifications

The app’s daily reminder is configured under Settings → Account. If it does not appear, enable notifications for gepard.fit in iOS or Android settings.

Delete an account

Sign in, open Settings → Account, choose “Delete my account,” and confirm with your email address. If you cannot sign in, contact support from the account email address.
